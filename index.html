A Story Rooted in Commerce

Four generations of family business shaped how I think about finance, margin, and building real value.

I grew up in Kolkata, India, in a family that has been in fresh produce trading for over 100 years. That foundation shaped my instinct for commerce. I studied Accounting & Finance at Jadavpur University, then moved to startup environments where that intuition became professional practice.

Now at BU Questrom, I'm teaching next-generation finance professionals as Summer TA at Training The Street.
